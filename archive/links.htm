The World Wide Web would not be what it is today if it weren't for links. If you can't find what you're looking for on my site, maybe you can find it elsewhere. Here is a collection of the most oft-visited Wolfenstein fan sites. Check them out if you're looking for levels, utilities, and other fun stuff.

Apogee Software Store: Buy Wolf3D
 Don't have the registered Wolfenstein? You can buy it online here for just 15 bucks, and download it right away -- no getting out of your chair to go hunting for it in some clumsy retail store. ;)

Barry's Wolfenstein 3-D Page
Barry Christian's Wolfy page, featuring game levels available for download, plus Barry's own projects, including the recently-released "Armageddon", a cool add-on with a hacked EXE and 60 new levels.

B.J. Blazkowicz's Hideout
A virtual cornucopia of Wolfy stuff, plus some things you may not have known!

Brandon Farmer's Source Code Help Center
If you need some more help or information on compiling your set of the Wolfenstein source code, Brandon has some good info and troubleshooting tips for first-time compilers. His message board (see below) can also be quite insightful!

ftp://ftp.std.com/customers3/src/pc/games/wolf3d/
 An ancient Wolf3D archive with some very hard-to-find files still languishing around, including Wolfenstein shareware version 1.1. Now that's archaic.

Gary's Wolfenstein 3-D Levels Homepage
 Gary's homepage has some really cool add-on levels for downloading, plus two of his own projects: Assassinate Hitler!, and my favorite, Schabbs 2000. Schabbs 2000 includes some cool EXE changes, which Gary accomplished all with a hex editor! Truly amazing!

Mr. Lowe's Wolfenstein 3-D Page
 A huge Wolfenstein site in the UK. Contains links to a lot of cool stuff, mainly add-on level packages. I selected some of the best from Mr. Lowe's to put in my add-ons section.

The Official Wolf2Duke Homepage
 Go here to download Wolf2Duke, Pär Sundbäck's total Wolfenstein conversion for Duke Nukem 3D. Highly recommended add-on for Duke and Wolfenstein fans alike! Final version now available!

The Official Wolfenstein 3-D FAQ
 This is the official Wolfenstein 3-D FAQ by Adam Williamson. It's a bit antiquated but contains everything you'd want to know about the game and its history.

THE Sliver's Wolfenstein 3D and Spear of Destiny Page
THE Silver's cool Wolfenstein page! There's tons of stuff here, including an online version of the Wolfenstein manual. Still a lot of work forthcoming.

Source Code Help Message Board
Owned by Brandon Farmer. Go here to find out what's up in the world of Wolfenstein source hacking. There's always people posting questions about how to do stuff, and sometimes there's even answers, too. ;)

Wolf3D World (Nate Smith's Wolfenstein Page)
 Nate Smith is the author of the highly-acclaimed Beyond Wolfenstein II. His site is home to BWII and Nate's other add-ons, plus some add-on reviews, source code info, and editorials.

WolfenDOOM Home Page
For you Doom players out there, here's a site filled with entire episodes based on the awesome WolfenDOOM TC for Doom. I've played several of these myself and they totally rock. The people (and resources) here seem to come mainly from a Macintosh background.

The Wolfenstein 3D Domain
The infamous Wolfenstein Domain, authored by D.J. Blazkowicz, Hamez, Spifferaneous, and Slayer. Home of the B.J. Blazkowicz Medal of Honor. These guys are getting a direct inside line from id Software themselves about the new Wolfenstein 3, possibly in development by an id affiliate...

The Wolfenstein 3D Fan Club
 The official fan club for our favorite game, easily one of the most popular fan clubs on the web. We're talking over 500 hits a day. If you're looking to find, chat about, or trade anything Wolfenstein, this is the place to do it!

WolfTrap's Source Code Tutorials
This guy deserves more credit (or mention) than he gets. There's lots of great info here for the budding Wolfenstein code writer. There's tutorials aplenty, pointers to where stuff is in the code, and other goodies. There's even a lesson on C++ basics! You can't go wrong with this stuff. Check it out.

The Wolfenstein 3-D Dome
Maintained and run by Brian Lowe and Tristan Van Putten, this has got to be one of the best sites out there, there is also a huge addons/tcs archive. This site is worth checking out.
