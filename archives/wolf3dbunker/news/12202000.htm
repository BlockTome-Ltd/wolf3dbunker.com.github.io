12/20/2000 - THE NEXT GENERATION OF THE BUNKER

Finally, after an extended period of stagnation, B.J.'s Wolfenstein Bunker has been revitalized -- and reengineered. The more contemporary design is also more functional, and a more focused approach has been taken with the site's content. Only the finest, hand-picked add-ons and materials will be posted on the Bunker, those that meet with the webmaster's approval. You can get Wolfenstein add-ons from anywhere, but the Bunker will be known as the source for the cream of the crop -- the place the demanding Wolfenstein player goes for a good game.

Old hands who have grown accustomed to the Bunker's layout will have some minor getting-used-to ahead of them, but I think you'll like the new additions. The Add-Ons section has been divided into three separate pages: TCs (stand-alone games), add-ons which require Wolfenstein, and add-ons which require Spear of Destiny. This way you can drill straight down to exactly what you're looking for. In addition, over time I will be putting up reviews for the add-ons I offer, as well as editorials on various Wolf-related topics (including information about the upcoming Wolfenstein sequel by Graymatter/Activision, Return to Castle Wolfenstein.

In project news, Project Totengraeber: Phase II has not been cancelled. It is still under construction, with approximately 12 levels left to design before project completion. Visit the Phase II News and Info page for the latest progress and screen shots. The sidebar to the right of the Newswire page will also provide a quick "snapshot" of the game's current state of production.

I hope you find the Bunker's new layout to your liking. In the coming weeks I'll also be putting up a form via which you can submit your own add-on for possible addition to the Bunker. I'm currently not accepting submissions just yet, but I will eventually.

Enjoy the site, and thanks to all of you out there for keeping the Wolfenstein legacy alive!

-B.J. Rowan
