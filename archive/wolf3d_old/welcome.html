Welcome to B.J. Rowan's Wolfenstein Bunker, the home for Wolfenstein fanatics in the 21st century. While you're here you don't have to be embarrassed to admit that you are still an avid fan (and modifier) of Wolfenstein with its original engine, in all its 16-bit glory. This site is meant to cater to all the game players who jumped on the First Person Shooter bandwagon back when it really started to move with Wolfenstein 3D in 1992, and can still appreciate the awesome game that Wolfenstein always was and always will be.

   I assume you know most everything about Wolfenstein and its characters, weapons, and levels. Rather than reiterate what everybody has been familiar with for going on 7 years now, I provide new or lesser-known Wolfenstein information, add-ons, user-created levels, and new source code developments. There's a whole new breed of Wolfenstein fanatics out here, and we're no longer content with just making levels! Now that Wolfenstein's source has been released to the public, people with enough programming experience can recompile their own versions of the game -- if they're one of the lucky few who can not only get the code to compile, but also get it to function flawlessly with their version of the game's resources. After years of trying, I finally got the code to compile successfully myself (see the Source Code section for information), and I have devoted this website to helping you other Wolfenstein fanatics compile it too.

   Feel free to use the nav-bar to the left to browse my site (or the text links below), and if you have any questions, Thanks for surfing in -- I hope you find what you're looking for!
