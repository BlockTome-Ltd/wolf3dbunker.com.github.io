Welcome to the Reviews Section, here you can find many reviews of TC's and custom add-on mission packs made by other Wolfenstein 3-D fans. Now you won't have to worry about downloading loads of missions and find the quality very low. Each mission is rated out of 5. Here are the basic terms for these numbers:

5 - Excellent/Distinction
4 - Very Good/Merit
3 - Good/Pass
2 - Satisfactory/Not too good
1 - Poor/Low quality
0 - Pathetic/Not even worth mentioning.

If you would like your addon reviewing please send me the files preferably in ZIP format, and I'll take a look. If I think it's a good addon, I will also add it for download to the addons/Tc section.
