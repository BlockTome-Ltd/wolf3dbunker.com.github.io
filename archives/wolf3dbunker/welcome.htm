Welcome to The Wolfenstein Bunker. The original haven for Wolfenstein source code developers on the Web, it is the premiere place for the true Wolfenstein aficionado. Those who appreciate the old-school atmosphere and depth of Wolfenstein will undoubtedly feel right at home.

Feel free to use the pull-down menus (or text panel below) to navigate through the site. Whether your interests lie with code hacking, top-notch level development tips, or just a plethora of custom missions to play, you'll be plenty satisfied with what the Bunker has to offer. And in the event that you crave still more, use the Links section as a jumpstation to the rest of the many sites in the Wolfenstein community.

If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the webmaster. Please, no requests for custom resources for your project. I give you the tools, but the talent must be all yours.
